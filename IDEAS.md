## Backlog

### Skills and gathering
1. Gathering sites - dedicated groves, fishing docks and herb gardens with richer nodes
2. Thieving - pickpocket town NPCs, steal from stalls, crack dungeon chests, angry guards
3. Agility - obstacle shortcuts (log balances, wall climbs, rope swings)
4. Farming - plant seeds in town patches, come back later to harvest
5. Runecrafting - make your own runes at altars (settles whether the Alchemist should stop selling runes)
6. Slayer - a slayer master who assigns kill tasks, points to spend on unlocks

### Combat and gear
7. Special attacks - a spec bar that fills over time, a special move per weapon
8. Poison and venom weapons, plus antipoison potions
9. Boss drop tables with a collection log of every unique found per boss
10. Set bonuses - full Barrows-style sets do something special
11. Ammo and rune pouches so they stop cluttering the inventory

### World and quests
12. Story quests - hand-made quest chains with dialogue
13. More towns with their own identity (desert trading post, dwarven mine town, elven village)
14. Random events on the road (lost child, travelling wizard, sandwich lady)
15. Boats between islands, with island-only resources
16. Weather that matters (rain boosts fishing, storms make sailing dangerous)

### Economy and social
17. Grand Exchange-style market with shifting prices
18. Bank tabs and search for the stash
19. Player house built with Construction (workshop, trophy room, portal chamber)

### Quality of life
20. Key rebinding on the Key Binds page
21. Right-click menus (Attack, Examine, Walk here, Use)
22. Minimap on the overworld and in towns
23. XP drops and a skill tracker
24. Settings page (volume, camera speed, click-to-walk speed, graphics quality)
25. Save export and import

### Polish
26. Level-up fireworks and jingle
27. Music tracks per area
28. Death gravestone to return to for your items
